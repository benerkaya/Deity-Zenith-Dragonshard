Deity Zenith - Dragonshard: everything in the Summon list (252 units incl. heroes, 39 buildings, 164 items)
Picker: Faction -> Category (Unit / Building / Item) -> Summon. Heroes are in the Unit list, marked "[Hero] ".
Items are not tied to a faction: Category = Item shows the same item list under every faction.
Each summon creates exactly the unit picked: a captain comes alone, without soldiers.
[captain] = Level Up (Level Up Key or Level Up Script) works on it (captain levels 1 to 6 in the game data; the upgrade buildings sell up to 5).
